Art, Algorithms, and Ownership:
Navigating Artist Rights & AI Data Mining

Comparing EU & US Legal Frameworks for Generative AI
Ruhi Anand Finn | ADAPT Centre, Trinity College Dublin
•
rufinn@tcd.ie
✨ Session Abstract & Motivation

Generative AI represents an existential inflection point for creative labor. As generative models rely heavily on vast datasets of human-created art for training, artists face unprecedented challenges regarding the unauthorized exploitation of visual styles, moral rights, and economic livelihoods.

Duration

90 Minutes Spotlight

Jurisdictions

EU, US, UK, Australia

Core Focus

TDM, Author Rights, AI Policy

📅 Tutorial Program & Agenda

Click any section below to inspect detailed topics, case laws, and timings.

1. Foundations of Artists' IP Rights & Comparative Frameworks 20 Mins (15m Pres + 5m Q&A)
  • Berne Convention: Core IP protections, moral rights vs economic rights.
  • EU Framework: CJEU Jurisprudence (Infopaq C-05/08, Levola Hengelo C-310/17, Funke Medien C-469/17, Cofemel C-683/17, Brompton Bicycle C-833/18), Copyright DSM Directive 2019/790, EU AI Act 2024/1689.
  • US Framework: Common law copyright, New York Times v. Microsoft (2025), Andy Warhol v. Goldsmith (2023), Fair Use & Transformative Use doctrines.
2. AI in the Creative Process & Judicial Review 20 Mins (15m Pres + 5m Q&A)
  • Four-Step Criteria: Domain Test, Human Authorship Requirement, Originality, and Expression.
  • Applying Tests to Hybrid AI Work: Qualified Person Test (Nova Productions v. Mazooma Games, Acohs v. Ucorp) and Originality standards (Painer C-145/10).
3. Training Data, Text & Data Mining (TDM), & Implications 20 Mins (15m Pres + 5m Q&A)
  • Ingestion Mechanics: Web crawling/scraping, Creative Commons licenses, Sui Generis database protections.
  • TDM Battlegrounds: EU TDM exceptions vs. US Fair Use defense. Case studies on LAION group and LLaMA training sets.
4. Interactive Policy Lab & Governance Brainstorm 30 Mins Group Activity
  • Quad Breakout Session: Attendees divide into 4 stakeholder groups to formulate governance blueprints.
  • Countermeasure Strategy: Developing opt-in frameworks, data provenance metadata standards, and equitable remuneration schemes.
